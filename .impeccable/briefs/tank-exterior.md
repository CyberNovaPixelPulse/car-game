# Exterior battle direction contract

Historical direction: the blind-driver and framed battle-layout requirements below describe #11/#25. [Issue #30](https://github.com/CyberNovaPixelPulse/car-game/issues/30) supersedes those rules with the current driver exterior view and full-viewport workstations. Use [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md) and the [current main surface brief](../surfaces/web-tank-main-ts.md) for the implemented layout; retain the accepted vehicle, environment and attachment quality direction below.

Mode: Experience for the world, Operate for the edge HUD. Authority: approved #11/#25 and the pinned 1944 Normandy/Caen direction. No new direction vote or generated comp. Code-led original 3D geometry plus verified CC0 physical surface maps.

THESIS: three people inhabit one heavy wartime machine. The gunner sees only the current gun direction, the machine-gunner independently scans from the hatch, the driver receives instruments without exterior pixels.

WORLD: stone farming villages, soil lanes and low stubble fields under natural cloud/daylight. M4A4 / Sherman V welded hull, short 75mm gun and rounded cast turret, VVSS wheel pairs; Panzer IV H long L48 gun, eight road wheels and segmented Schürzen; Tiger I broad hull, overlapping large wheels, distinct turret and 88mm L56. Human-sized hatches, vision slits, deck grilles, tow gear and separate bare steel. No insignia or fictional authentic unit claims.

FIRST VIEWPORT: in combat, the large room introduction recedes. At 1280×720 gunner shares scene, centered reticle, hull/gun directions, HP and chamber state in the same viewport. Real enemy warning remains on the battlefield; rules do not move into a fake period control panel. Clear room forms remain unchanged before battle. Narrow viewports preserve readable flow but do not add touch play.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: physical displacement/turn from authority drives left/right track motion; independent turret and pitch pivots keep gun geometry and view pointing the same way. Stopping freezes running gear. Normal cover/aim/fire encounters demonstrate the same rules before and after art replacement.

COLLISION: original solid cover bounds remain the source. Masonry fills those boxes; the in-bounds store roof is inside its stone parapet. Taller vegetation and pitched-roof background farms remain outside playable bounds. Low grass is decorative. No additional blockers, AI changes or damage changes.

QUALITY BAR: explicit user decision on 2026-11-07: 「接受目前簡化外觀，修正浮空零件後完成整合」. The current simplified vehicle surfaces, Browning finish, worked field rows and foliage are accepted; do not reopen the earlier realistic-material/vegetation rebuild requirement. Keep distinguishable vehicle silhouettes, Sherman six road wheels / three VVSS pairs, natural-light readability and role restrictions. Mechanical attachments must sit on their modeled supporting surface: cupola fasteners follow the actual offset hatch cap and Sherman lamps are bracketed to the sloped armour. This acceptance is a changed exterior finish criterion, not a claim that the original realism criterion passed.

Evidence required: final actual browser gunner desktop and 1280×720, MG laptop, blind driver, normal enemy/cover/fire captures, model close-up QA from an external non-shipping viewer only. Asset hashes/metadata and historical research recorded in public/art. Review all material scope and never use showroom art as gameplay proof.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
