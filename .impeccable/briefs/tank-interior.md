# Loader interior and machine-gun feedback

Authority: approved #11 and #26; 1944 Normandy, 75mm Sherman V / M4A4 visual reference. Experience scene with Operate HUD. This is a game, not an exact historical operating reconstruction; three crew, blind driver and ejection stun are deliberately fictional rules.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The loader works inside a substantial steel machine. The enclosed breech and the moving round carry the view, while each operation reads from the same authority snapshot as the gunner's cannon.

OWN-WORLD: Muted painted steel, restrained wear, exposed machined surfaces and brass ammunition under local work lights. Existing PingFang TC / Noto Sans TC text and charcoal/green/amber operational palette remain consistent with the room.

STORY: Select a round, hold R, resolve a jam if necessary, hear and see the latch, coordinate fire, then duck below the ejection path. Tab changes workplace without changing the loaded shell or weapon progress.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact station and health at the top; mode, ejection warning and posture immediately beneath. The enclosed scene dominates. Loading status sits in a compact strip below the scene, jam track sits immediately above the scene while active and keyboard instructions sit directly below. No exterior geometry is created for this view; driver remains without an exterior renderer.

FORM: User-pinned realistic WWII direction; no new concept election. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
